Honest limits

Geometry is schematic. Lift, endurance, and hover are not demonstrated. Next real number is a tethered force-stand reading, not a render.

Nature principles

Insects fly in a Reynolds-number band where steady airfoil theory under-predicts lift. Useful ideas are mechanisms, not brand names and not a particular commercial drone.

Unsteady lift

  • Leading-edge vortex (LEV) — during a translational stroke a vortex can sit over the wing and keep suction after a fixed wing would stall.
  • Added mass / wake capture — starting and reversing the stroke does work on the air; some of that comes back on the next half-stroke.
  • Clap-and-fling (Weis-Fogh) — an optional high-lift start when wings peel apart. Not assumed for Flyer-0; treat as a later experiment, not a default.

Structure

  • Vein + membrane — stiffness where bending moments are high (leading edge, root); film elsewhere. We copy the layout, not chitin.
  • Passive twist — a slightly compliant wing can shed peak torque. That is a materials choice, not a control law.
  • Four-wing planform — dragonfly-like: independent fore/hind phasing is a later luxury. First bench can lock phase.

Mass, muscle, sensing — what does not transfer

  • Insect flight muscle is an energy-dense, high-cycle actuator with its own heat and elastic storage. We do not have an equivalent.
  • Dipteran halteres are elegant rate gyros. We will use a cheap IMU because we can buy one, not because it is biologically faithful.
  • Dragonfly flight is vision-heavy. Optical flow is a candidate sensor, not a promise of insect-level closed-loop performance.

This model’s long abdomen and paired wings are a planform hint (pitch inertia, some yaw authority). They are not a claim that the robot “is” a dragonfly.

Tech mimic routes

Pick the cheapest path that can fail on the bench. Do not optimize for a brochure.

First-build bias: one motor pair + four-bar (or Scotch yoke) driving both sides in phase, carbon spar + PET/Mylar film, USB tether for power. Independence, piezo, and onboard LiPo come after the wing survives a frequency sweep.

Materials

  • Spars — pultruded carbon or printed sandwich. Leading edge takes the LEV load path.
  • Membrane — PET, Mylar, or thin PU. Wrinkles are data: they show where the vein map is wrong.
  • Thorax — printed bay for motors, ESC, and a dummy mass where the battery will sit. Keep CG marked.
  • Legs / skids — crash structure. Insects fold; we will break replaceable rods first.

Power & control

  • Tether first — USB or bench PSU through a thin umbilical. Flight time is not a milestone until lift > weight on a stand.
  • LiPo later — 1S/2S only after the current waveform is boring. Energy density is the constraint, not a slogan.
  • Open-loop flap → stroke amplitude / offset → IMU attitude loop. No vision stack until hover-on-a-line is dull.
  • Do not copy a vendor’s airframe, linkage patent story, or claimed minutes of flight. Measure ours.

Bench milestones

A path, not a calendar. Each gate is a measurement. Skip a gate and the next one lies.

M0 — Schematic freeze · this page

Lock the story: four-wing schematic, borrowed vs chosen, and the test order. Pass: someone else can explain the plan from this viewer without extra slides.

Gate: published /insect/ schematic + path copy.

M1 — Tethered flap

Airframe bolted to a block. Sweep frequency and stroke. Watch spars, film, and bearings. Log motor current vs Hz.

Gate: N continuous minutes at a chosen Hz with no glue/film failure and a repeatable current trace. Lift is not required.

M2 — Force stand / lift attempt

Same fixture, now on a scale or load cell. Cycle-average vertical force vs weight budget. Change amplitude and CG dummy masses.

Gate: cycle-average lift ≥ weight or a written reason (area, Hz, power) and the next cut. Hover is still forbidden.

M3 — Controlled hover attempt

Safety lines or a sliding gantry. Closed-loop pitch/roll on the IMU. Expect crashes; design skids to be cheap.

Gate: several seconds of attitude-hold without hitting the hard stop, repeated on another day. Not a demo video as the only evidence.

M4 — Free flight (netted bay)

Umbilical off only if M3 is boring. Soft walls, spotter, pack disconnect on the bench checklist.

Gate: a short, intentional hop with a logged attitude trace — then land or crash without a fire. Endurance claims wait.

Still open (do not invent)

  • Real mass, wing area after first film cut, and motor Kv.
  • Whether fore/hind phase is worth a second linkage.
  • Whether optical flow is needed before M3 or only after.
