Not a free win

Piezo does not automatically beat rotary + spring. Resonant lock-in fights you when you want kinematics. HV conversion fights you on mass and heat. Payload is small. Neighbor starts: Flyer-0 (rotary + deliberate elastic return at ~15 g) and Flyer-1 (independent wing pairs).

Path to prototype

A resonant-flexure path. Public cards only. No invented Hz or lift numbers.

P0 — Resonant architecture · open

Stack, amplifier geometry, and wing as one tuned structure. No crank in the sketch.

Exit: the flexure amp is specified, and everyone agrees there is no rotary fallback hiding in the drawing.

P1 — Coupon + HV bench

Drive a dummy flexure on the bench. Learn voltage, stroke, and heat before a wing exists.

Exit: the stack moves the amp without cracking, and the HV path is boring.

P2 — Resonant sweep

Wing on the amp. Sweep drive frequency. Find the peak — and how narrow it is.

Exit: a repeatable resonance, not a one-off lucky flap.

P3 — Tethered micro

Soft tether / umbilical. First question is survive-the-resonance, not free flight.

Exit: tethered flap that stays on resonance without cooking the stack.

P4 — Demo ready

Repeatable micro/resonant research demo.

Exit: someone else can run the HV + sweep checklist.
