# What changed for the Layout A clearance

- Straights and corners are carved for a 17.5 mm ball, including the tongue where the next piece drops away.
- Split, merge, switch, DROP40, and the inlet adapter are carved on their centrelines. DROP40 is one guided slope.
- The switch gate has a down pose that blocks branch B and an up pose that clears both balls. Each detent is about 7.7 mm³.
- Posts that meet a track by more than 8 mm³ are omitted.
- The helix stays at radius 90 mm and pitch 80 mm. Layout B is labeled a design study because that coil meets the lift.
- The viewer counts STL families from the manifest. The full-kit download stays held.
