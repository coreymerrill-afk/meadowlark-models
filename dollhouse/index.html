Grand dollhouse kit

Six manors · one snap system · 1:24 · Bambu Lab H3C

Drag to rotate · scroll/pinch to zoom · right-drag to pan
Light channels (Ø3.2)Wire runs (Ø5)Magnetic service panels

Press Explode in Explore and the house lifts apart in build order:

  1. Garden base, floor tiles
  2. Ground-story walls and corner posts
  3. Porches, bays, arcades, balconies
  4. Upper stories, one level at a time
  5. Roof panels, turret caps, dormers
  6. Chimneys, cupolas, finials, spires, weathervanes

Every ornament pegs into the same Ø6.3 sockets on the 31.75 mm grid, so turrets, porches and roofs move between houses.

Assembly (every house)

  1. Print the snap clearance strip first and pick the socket that pushes in firm and pulls out by hand.
  2. Lay floor tiles, dowels, corner posts, then walls (same as v1).
  3. Peg in the ornaments: porch posts and railings, bay windows, turret drums.
  4. Stack upper floor tiles on the corner-post dowels, repeat walls.
  5. Drop roof panels onto ridge dowels, cap the turrets, then add chimneys, cupolas and finials.

Accessories match the storybook look. Floor items have Ø5.9 pegs; lamps have LED pockets and Ø3.2 wire bores.
