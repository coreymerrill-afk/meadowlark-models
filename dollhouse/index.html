Press Explode in Explore and the house lifts apart in build order:

  1. Garden / outdoor modules, floor tiles
  2. Ground-story walls and corner posts (rear face open for play)
  3. Porches and wide doorways
  4. Upper stories
  5. Lift-off roof panels
  6. Optional chimneys / cupolas

Every outdoor module pegs into the same Ø6.3 sockets on the 31.75 mm grid — rearrange the yard like Legos.

Assembly (every house)

  1. Print the snap clearance strip first and pick the socket that pushes in firm and pulls out by hand.
  2. Lay floor tiles, dowels, corner posts, then walls — leave the back face open (or use open_face_rail_v4).
  3. Use wall_panel_clear_window_v4 and wall_panel_wide_door_v4 for sightlines and hand access between rooms.
  4. Stack upper floors; drop roof_panel_lift_v4 on last so a kid can lift it off to play.
  5. Snap outdoor modules (lawn, path, fence, gate, sandbox, shed…) onto yard tiles and rearrange.
