Assembly

  1. Print the clearance strip first. Pick the socket where a pin pushes in firmly and pulls out by hand.
  2. Lay the four floor tiles in a square, switch stubs facing where you want room switches.
  3. At each outer corner, push a dowel pin into the corner socket and drop a corner post on it.
  4. Seat the short walls: corner end against the post, posts into the tile sockets. Two walls meet on each tile seam.
  5. Run LEDs or wire before the walls go on: floor loop → corner riser → up the post → wall-top channel.

Lights

  • Low voltage only — coin cell, AA pack, or USB 5 V. The channels fit 3 mm LEDs, fiber, or thin (30 AWG) wire.
  • Each room has one switch pocket on a tile edge, sized for a micro slide or tact switch.
  • One outside switch point feeds porch and corner lamps.

Snap fit tip

FDM holes print a little small. If pins are too tight, use the next bigger socket from the strip; if they wobble, go one smaller. Parts should snap apart without tools.
