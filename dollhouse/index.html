Press Explode in Explore to lift each layer apart in build order:

  1. Floor tiles (story 1)
  2. Dowel pins + corner posts
  3. Walls
  4. Furniture, fixtures, people
  5. Upper floor tiles, then the story-2 posts and walls (two-story houses)

Every house uses the same parts. A layout is just a different arrangement of tiles, posts and walls, so pieces move between houses.

Assembly (all houses)

  1. Print the snap clearance strip first. Choose the socket where a pin pushes in firmly and still pulls out by hand.
  2. Lay out the floor tiles. Point the switch stubs where you want room switches.
  3. Push a dowel into each corner socket and drop a corner post on it. The corner post owns the corner socket.
  4. Seat the walls. Use short walls (107 mm) next to a corner, full walls (127 mm) mid-run, and mini walls (87 mm) on a one-tile side with corners at both ends.
  5. Two-story houses: upper floor tiles drop onto the dowels on top of the corner posts. The stair module pegs into a ground-floor socket.

Accessories share the 1:24 scale (an adult figure is 73 mm, about 6 ft). Bases that sit on the floor have Ø5.9 pegs that drop into the tile sockets, so nothing slides around.
