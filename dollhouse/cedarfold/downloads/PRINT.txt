UNPRINTED PROTOTYPE. Plastic print STLs only.
Not a toy-safety certification. Not a finished kit. Not a child-safe toy.
Hinge pinch, tipping, a drawer coming free, and caster-brake loads were not tested.
Clearances are CAD allowances.
The visual design is an initial simplified original prototype.
Reference images inspire features. This is not a finished replica.

PRINTING LIMITATIONS
The CAD generator did not run a slicer.
A later independent audit ran a generic PrusaSlicer 2.7.4 profile: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.12 mm layers, PLA.
That audit is not this generator, not an H3C profile, and not a real print.
Hinge coupon A and the drawer rail coupon sliced with no support islands.
The hearth ground storey, in the supplied pose, needed 865 support islands and an estimate of about 50 hours.
Support counts and time estimates are illustrative and depend on slicer settings.
Orientation and how parts are split still need further development.
Bases and drawers are supplied on edge. Whether that pose is suitable was not validated.
A 0.6 mm or 0.8 mm nozzle was not tested and is not a proven fix.

Intended shell rule: broad back on the bed, keys toward print -Y, cornice lace toward print +Y.
Do not stand a shell on its shortest axis.
The base and drawer files do not follow a validated pose. See above.
Metal rod, spring pin, casters, and screws are not in this zip.
Print cf_coupon_hinge_a/b and the drawer coupon before the house.
Drawer CAD travel 51.5 mm.
Hinge pin Ø4 mm, bore flat clearance 0.35 mm.
