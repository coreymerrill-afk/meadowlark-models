# Cedarfold Playhouse v1.0.0

Original 1:24 folding cottage for the Bambu Lab H3C. Unprinted prototype. The visual design is an initial simplified original. Reference images inspire features. This is not a finished replica.

It is not the Queen Anne Playhouse and not one of the six play-kit manors. The geometry is generated by `cad/generate.py`. Style references suggested a warm open-front wooden house, drawers under the rooms, and a hinged body on wheels. The plan, stairs, and silhouette are this model's own.

## Mechanisms, as drawn

- Two wings. The hearth wing stays put. The garden wing folds from 0° (open, fronts toward you) to −180° (closed, garden behind the hearth) on a vertical pin at the shared rear corner.
- Knuckles alternate in Z. CAD radial clearance to the bore flats is 0.35 mm. Axial knuckle gap is 0.5 mm. A rear shroud is drawn on each knuckle. Pinch was not tested.
- Each wing has its own base and drawer. Travel is 51.55 mm. A stop sits 0.45 mm past that. The drawer lifts out of the groove only at full extension.
- Eight schematic 25 mm plate casters. Four are the brake pair. Screws are M4 × 12, catalog only.
- Storeys stack on rectangular keys. No magnets.
- Each wing has a 42 mm stair, 12 risers, 8.167 mm rise, 7.5 mm tread. The landing is the back hall, 44 mm deep, solid and continuous with the floor. The flight is an opening in the floor above.
- 5 V lighting is an empty back-wall groove plus a rear slack pocket. No wire is included.

## Print

Preferred part margin 310 × 300 × 315 mm. Nominal bed 330 × 320 × 325 mm. Nozzle basis 0.4 mm. A 0.2 mm nozzle is not confirmed.

Shells are filed with the broad back toward the bed. Keys point toward print −Y. Cornice lace points toward print +Y. Do not stand a shell on its shortest axis. The cap lies on its side so print Z is the diameter. Bases and drawers are supplied on edge. That pose was not validated.

Meshes are snapped to a 0.01 mm grid so the float32 STL reloads as one watertight body. That snap is smaller than the design gaps. It is not a measured print.

The generator did not run a slicer. A later independent audit used generic PrusaSlicer 2.7.4, a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.12 mm layers, and PLA. Hinge coupon A and the drawer rail coupon had no support islands. The hearth ground storey in the supplied pose needed 865 support islands and an estimate of about 50 hours. That slice is not an H3C profile and not a real print. The numbers are illustrative and configuration-dependent. A 0.6 mm or 0.8 mm nozzle was not tested. This is not a toy-safety certification.

## Regenerate

```bash
python3 dollhouse/cedarfold/cad/generate.py
```

Needs Python 3 with `manifold3d`, `numpy`, `trimesh`, `scipy`, and `Pillow`.
