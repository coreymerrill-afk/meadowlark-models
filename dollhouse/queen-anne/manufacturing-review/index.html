What changed

Both drawers pull out from the open rear. Side guides and removable travel stops are included in the mechanical design. Oversized sections are split with alignment sockets. Lighting channels avoid the stair opening and remain on the fixed frame. Wheels are purchased-hardware references and are excluded from the print kit.

Still untested: slicer toolpaths, supports, thin ornament durability, printed sliding fit, adhesive joint strength, caster stability and the electrical assembly. No furniture is included.
