A frame table for the X1 Ultra

Two long 2×6 rails, joists every 16 inches, six 4×4 legs, a lower rail that ties the legs, and a plywood top. You and a helper build the frame upside down, flip it, then screw the top on.

Why it does not rack or sag

The machine, the bed, and the sheet sit on the plywood, the plywood sits on 2×6 joists every 16 inches, the joists sit in two long rails, and those rails sit on six 4×4 legs that run straight down to the levelers and the slab. The rails are on edge, and the long sheet seam lands on a joist, so the plywood is not asked to bridge a wide gap by itself. The plywood top ties the frame into one stiff sheet, the lower rail loop ties all six legs, and the end panels keep the ends from leaning. When it is standing, push the top sideways with about 50 lb; add a plywood panel on the back or two diagonals only if it moves.

Tools: a circular saw and a straightedge, a drill, a socket, a level, clamps, and sawhorses. A table saw is not required. The full list is on Shop & cut list.

Shop & cut list

Nominal lumber is smaller than the name. A 2×4 is 1½ × 3½, a 2×6 is 1½ × 5½, a 4×4 is 3½ × 3½, and ¾ plywood is 23/32, about 0.72 in thick.

Legs are 32.78 in if the leveler is 2.5 in. Dry-stack your real leveler before cutting. At that length only two legs fit in an 8 ft 4×4, so buy 3 posts.

Tools

  • Circular saw and a straightedge or a track saw. A table saw is not required.
  • Speed square, framing square, tape, pencil.
  • Drill/driver, plus an impact driver if you have one. Bits: ⅛ in pilot, ⅜ in for the bolts, countersink.
  • Socket for the nuts (9/16 in for a ⅜ nut).
  • A 4 ft level and a 2 ft level. Four clamps. Two or three sawhorses.
  • Caulk gun if you use the adhesive. Safety glasses, hearing protection, dust mask.
  • Optional: miter saw, orbital sander, a few shims.

Site

  • Coated polyaspartic concrete slab. Pads under every foot. Lift, do not drag.
  • About 48 in of clear aisle along one long side, so a full sheet can pass.
  • Room for two people to flip a 111 in frame.
  • Power for the saw and the drill is a normal shop outlet. The machine’s own power draw is still unknown, so do not size a new circuit yet.

Safety

  • The flip is about 150 lb, and a sheet is about 70 lb. Two people. Lift with your legs.
  • Glasses, hearing protection, and a mask when you cut. Support the offcut.
  • Keep hands out of the line of the blade. Unplug the saw when you change the blade.
  • The metal sheet under the honeycomb is a fire break. Follow the machine’s own instructions when it arrives.

Floor and space

The shop floor is a coated polyaspartic concrete slab. Point loads from the levelers are fine on that slab. Pads go under every foot, and the table gets lifted, not dragged. Leave about 48 in along one long side so a sheet can come in. The machine’s power draw is still unknown.

If a board is twisted, or the levelers do not match

  • A twisted 2×6 can be a joist, crown up, if the screws pull it to the line. If it will not sit, set it aside. Do not make it a long rail.
  • If your leveler is not 2.5 in at mid-travel, recut the legs. Leg length is the top height, minus 0.72 in of plywood, minus the leveler you measured. Do not stack washers under a short leg.
  • If one corner of the slab is low, use the leveler thread. Keep some thread engaged both ways. You have about an inch of adjustment.

Wood movement

Construction lumber moves as it dries in the shop. After about a month, check the level, tighten the bolts, and turn the levelers again. The frame needs to be level and not rocking. The bed can be shimmed if the top is a little off.

CNC, later

Build the stand and the laser bed together. When you do run the CNC spindle, one sacrificial MDF or plywood sheet on the honeycomb is enough. That layer can wait until you are actually cutting with the spindle.

Changing the height

The top height is one number in this file: TOP_H = 36, near the top of the script. Change that, and the leg length, the end panels, and the 4×4 count on this page follow it. It stays provisional until the machine’s bed height is known.
