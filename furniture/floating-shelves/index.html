Floating Shelf Set on French Cleats

A set of hollow floating shelves, each hung on a concealed aluminum Z-bar French cleat, stepped in length. Off the floor entirely, so the robot vacuum is unaffected.

900 mm wide, 220 mm deep, 680 mm tall (35.4 × 8.7 × 26.8 in). Shelf lengths 900, 750, 600 mm. Each shelf box 80 mm tall. 1 sheet. Mid retail price $210, an estimate. Typical machine time 1.9 h.

Build stages

  1. 1

    Cut the sheet(s) about 195 min (3 h 15 min)

    Run the fit-test coupon first (slot = tab + 0.1 mm), then cut 1 sheet(s) from the DXFs (CUT layer): 29 m of path, 18 parts. Inside-out order, air assist on.

  2. 2

    Remove char and sand about 72 min (1 h 12 min)

    Brush the char off every edge, 120 then 180 grit. Ease the TOP front and side edges (the visible top slab); sand the slot ends flush later, after glue.

  3. 3

    Dry fit one shelf about 30 min

    For each shelf: stand the two END plates, drop the FRONT and HANGER between them, then fit BOTTOM underneath and TOP on top of all the tabs. Check the box is square and the cavity is clear for the Z-bar.

  4. 4

    Pre-finish about 65 min (1 h 5 min)

    Stain or seal the inside of the box and the HANGER rear face lightly, then finish TOP/BOTTOM faces. Mask the tabs and slots so the glue still bonds.

  5. 5

    Glue-up (Titebond III) about 60 min (1 h)

    Per shelf: glue the ENDs into BOTTOM, add FRONT and HANGER, then seat TOP over every tab. Clamp with cauls across the width. Wipe squeeze-out.

  6. 6

    Screws as clamps about 24 min

    6 #8 x 1-1/2 in screws (3.0 mm pilots cut in the BOTTOM): from the underside up into the END plates, between the tabs. The TOP is glue and tab only, so the top face stays clean; clamp it with cauls while it dries.

  7. 7

    Fit the Z-bar cleats about 45 min

    Screw the shelf half of each aluminum Z-bar (lengths 24/24/18 in) to the rear face of the HANGER with #8 x 5/8 in screws (shorter than the 18 mm plate, so they cannot show). Mount the wall half level into studs (2 screws per stud, 16 in centers), then hang the shelf from above.

  8. 8

    Hang, test, pack about 25 min

    Hang, then load-test each shelf with 10 kg at the front edge. Flat-pack in one 1.0 x 0.3 x 0.1 m box.

About 8 h 36 min for all 8 stages.

Safety notes

  • Mount the wall half of each cleat into studs only (not drywall anchors alone); level it before hanging.
  • Rated target 10 kg per shelf at a 220 mm depth, UNVERIFIED - load test each shelf at 15 kg before selling; the rating falls as depth grows.
  • Keep heavy items (books, appliances) on a conventional bookcase, not on a floating shelf.
  • Not a children's product (no CPSIA testing); do not use as a climbing aid.

Unverified / to test

  • Machine time is an estimate (120 W diode on 18 mm ply, sourced range, not measured on this machine); cut time and profit/hour depend on it.
  • Z-bar cleat load rating and the pull-out strength of the shelf-half screws in an 18 mm HANGER face are not tested; the checks.txt load-path line is a rough hand estimate.
  • Z-bar profile dimensions differ by supplier (the 24 mm recess and 44 mm cavity are sized for a typical 1 in bar and must be confirmed with the bar you buy).
  • The wall bar is aluminum from a hardware store: the laser cannot cut a true 45 deg bevel in ply, so ply-only cleats are not offered.
  • The cost model charges one whole 4x8 sheet but this set fills only 39% of it (offcut not credited). Nesting 2 sets per sheet (area allows it; not nested here) would cut mid cost to about $110 and lift mid gross margin from 26% to about 48%. The Z-bar hardware (about $36) is a large share of cost. As modeled, the low price band is below cost.
