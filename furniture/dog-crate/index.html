Build stages

  1. 1

    Cut the sheets about 412 min (6 h 52 min)

    Fit-test coupon first (slot = tab + 0.1 mm); cut 2 sheet(s): 62 m of path with 30 rounded vent/slat openings. Use cabinet-grade ply with NAUF/CARB2 glue (no MDF - dogs chew it and moisture swells it). Inside-out order, air assist on.

  2. 2

    Remove char, sand, round every edge about 120 min (2 h)

    Brush the char off, 120 then 180 then 220 grit. Hand-round ALL edges, slot ends and the inside of the door opening (about 3 mm) - no splinters or sharp arrises where a dog can rub. Sand the tab ends flush later.

  3. 3

    Dry fit about 20 min

    Stand SIDE-L/R, slide in FLOOR, FRONT and BACK, then drop TOP over every tab. Check the DOOR sits in its opening with about 2 mm all round and the trays drop in.

  4. 4

    Pre-finish (pet-safe) about 75 min (1 h 15 min)

    Finish every part before glue-up with a cured pet-safe finish (water-based poly or food-safe hardwax oil, 2-3 coats); let it cure fully (3-7 days is best) before a dog goes in. Mask tabs and slots.

  5. 5

    Glue-up (Titebond III) about 35 min

    Glue FLOOR, FRONT and BACK into SIDE-L, add SIDE-R, then seat TOP on all tabs. Clamp across the width and check it is square. Wipe squeeze-out; no glue on the door or trays.

  6. 6

    Screws from the outside only about 20 min

    12 #8 x 1-1/2 in screws (3.0 mm pilots pre-cut) through the side faces into the floor, front and back edges. Nothing protrudes inside the crate. Countersink and plug.

  7. 7

    Hang the door about 30 min

    Two 3 in surface-mount flush hinges on the right: pre-drill 2.5 mm for the hinge screws, set the door with 2 mm gaps (never wider than 4 mm - paw safety). Fit a dog-proof barrel bolt on the door and drop its pin through the 9 mm hole in the header.

  8. 8

    Sand tab ends and final finish about 40 min

    Sand the tab ends on the tabletop flush, final coat on the top (wipe-clean, heat/water resistant poly), edge-seal the cut ends.

  9. 9

    Pad, test, pack about 30 min

    Lay a washable pad on the trays. Shake test, then flat-pack: parts up to 900 x 600 mm in one box with corner guards; include hinge and bolt hardware and the care and safety sheet.

About 13 h 2 min for all 9 stages.

Safety notes

  • Animal safety (design intent, NOT certified): all openings <= 38 mm, every slot end rounded, door gap about 2 mm, no screws or hinge hardware inside, tabs flush.
  • Use a dog-proof latch and test with the dog supervised; this is a rest crate, not a restraint or vehicle crate.
  • Pet-safe cured finish only; no MDF, no chemical stains inside; ventilation about 30 slotted openings.
  • Interior 824 x 484 x 536 mm: check the dog's shoulder height + 50 mm, nose-to-tail length + 80 mm before ordering; the default suits a dog up to about 18 kg.
  • Tabletop rating target 25 kg of objects (UNVERIFIED); the crate floor target 40 kg dog (UNVERIFIED).

Unverified / to test

  • Not tested with an animal; rating and chew resistance unknown.
  • Hinge and bolt positions are guidance; screw holes for hinges are drilled by hand.
  • Machine time is an estimate (120 W diode on 18 mm ply, sourced range, not measured on this machine); cut time and profit/hour depend on it.
