Rolling Kitchen Cart

A rolling cart for toasters and panini presses: 13 slot rows for shelves on a 50 mm pitch, an optional drawer, locking casters, and one dense 4x8 sheet.

600 mm wide, 450 mm deep, 900 mm tall (23.6 × 17.7 × 35.4 in). Casters lift the base 102 mm. 13 shelf rows, 50 mm apart. 1 sheet. Mid retail price $280, an estimate.

Build stages

  1. 1

    Cut the sheet about 334 min (5 h 34 min)

    Cut the fit-test coupon first (slot = tab + 0.1 mm), then the 29-part sheet from the DXF (CUT layer): 50 m of path. The cart is the densest nest: expect a long run, so check the sheet is flat and weighted before starting.

  2. 2

    Remove char and sand about 60 min (1 h)

    Brush the char off every edge, sand 120 then 180 grit, ease the worktop edge and the shelf fronts by hand.

  3. 3

    Dry fit about 20 min

    Stand SIDE-L and SIDE-R on BASE, add BACK, RAILs, the worktop, and slide in a SHELF on its LEDGERs. Check the shelf-slot pitch (50 mm) and that the tabs end flush.

  4. 4

    Pre-finish about 60 min (1 h)

    Seal every face now, the worktop most of all (kitchen use: water-based poly, 3 coats on top). Mask the tabs and slots so the glue bonds to bare wood.

  5. 5

    Feet and casters about 30 min

    Glue and screw the FOOT-A and FOOT-B pairs under the base (8 x #8 x 1-3/4 in), then bolt the 4 casters on the 73.2 x 44.5 mm pattern with M8 bolts and nuts. Fit at least 2 locking casters.

  6. 6

    Glue-up (Titebond III) about 35 min

    Glue SIDE-L and SIDE-R onto BASE, the BACK, then the three RAILs, then set the worktop over the side tabs. Clamp across the width and check diagonals.

  7. 7

    Screws as clamps about 30 min

    45 #8 screws in all: #8 x 1-1/2 in through the side faces and top; #8 x 1-1/4 in for rails and shelf locks. Pilots are laser-cut at 3.0 mm.

  8. 8

    Shelves and ledgers about 20 min

    Screw a LEDGER pair into the slot row you choose, then drop in the SHELF and lock it with the 2 SHELF-LOCK screws. Move rows in 50 mm steps for tall appliances.

  9. 9

    Optional drawer and cleat about 45 min

    Drawer: glue the finger-jointed box, drop in the bottom, screw DRW-FACE, set on its DRAW ledgers. Cleat: glue the two CLEAT-BACKER layers to the back and fit the Z-bar. A loaded cart is not wall-hung: use the cleat for an empty/tidy-storage version only.

  10. 10

    Finish, load test, pack about 40 min

    Final coat, 24 h cure, a 40 kg load test rolled across a doorstep, lock check on a slope, then flat-pack.

About 11 h 14 min for all 10 stages.

Safety notes

  • Locking casters: lock at least two on any loaded or worktop use.
  • Cart load rating is untested: target 40 kg shelves + 20 kg worktop, UNVERIFIED. Load test every design change.
  • Tip: the cart is 900 mm tall and narrow; keep heavy appliances on the lowest shelves and do not roll over thresholds loaded.
  • Do not run heat-producing appliances on the ply unprotected: use a trivet.
  • Not a children's product (no CPSIA testing).

Unverified / to test

  • Laser feed 100-200 mm/min is an estimate.
  • Load and tip behaviour untested.
  • Price band is my assumption (cart is not in SHORTLIST.md).
  • Caster price and fit are from the original design; the hole pattern is assumed for common 3 in plate casters.
  • Depth and width ranges were build-checked only at the stress variants (400-520 mm deep, 480-800 mm wide).
