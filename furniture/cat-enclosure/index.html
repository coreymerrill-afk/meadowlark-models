Build stages

  1. 1

    Cut the sheet(s) about 212 min (3 h 32 min)

    Run the fit-test coupon first (slot = tab + 0.1 mm), then cut 1 sheet(s) from the DXFs (CUT layer): 32 m of path, 36 vent slots. Inside-out order, air assist on.

  2. 2

    Remove char and sand about 50 min

    Brush char from every edge, 120 then 180 grit; round over the cat-door edge and the lid edge by hand so there is nothing sharp at whisker height.

  3. 3

    Dry fit about 15 min

    Stand SIDE-L, push in BOTTOM, FRONT and BACK, add SIDE-R, then set the LID (with keys loose) on top. Check the litter box slides in and out with the lid off, and the door is square. No glue.

  4. 4

    Seal the inside about 75 min (1 h 15 min)

    Litter and urine are the enemy of bare ply: give every inside face and the BOTTOM top 3 coats of waterborne poly or a 2-part epoxy, masking the tabs and slots. A removable plastic tray under the box is a cheap second layer.

  5. 5

    Glue-up (Titebond III) about 30 min

    Glue BOTTOM, FRONT and BACK into SIDE-L, add SIDE-R and clamp with cauls across the width. Check square by measuring the diagonals. Wipe squeeze-out at once.

  6. 6

    Screws as clamps about 20 min

    16 #8 screws (3.0 mm pilots cut in): #8 x 1-1/2 in through the side faces into BOTTOM, FRONT and BACK; the lid keys take 4 x #8 x 1-1/4 in down through the LID (plug the heads).

  7. 7

    Lid keys about 20 min

    Glue the two LID-KEY strips under the LID so they sit 1 mm inside the walls (use the carcass as the jig), screw from the top, plug and sand. The lid then lifts off by hand for litter-box access.

  8. 8

    Finish, test, pack about 45 min

    Two coats of low-VOC water-based poly on the outside, 48 h cure and air out before it goes near a cat. Check a 15 kg lid load. Felt pads under the 4 feet. Flat-pack in one box about 0.7 x 0.6 x 0.12 m.

About 7 h 47 min for all 8 stages.

Safety notes

  • Pet safety: no gaps a paw or head can wedge into (door is a smooth arch with no latch); keep the lid heavy enough to stay put (the lid weighs about 3 kg).
  • Seal the inside with a waterproof finish; urine soaks bare ply. Let VOCs off-gas fully (48 h minimum) before use.
  • Rated target 15 kg on the lid (a lamp and a plant), UNVERIFIED - do not sit on it.
  • Ventilation: keep the side vents unblocked. Not a children's product (no CPSIA testing).
  • Low piece (under 27 in): no wall anchor needed.

Unverified / to test

  • Machine time is an estimate (120 W diode on 18 mm ply, sourced range, not measured on this machine); cut time and profit/hour depend on it.
  • Litter box sizes vary a lot: measure the exact box before ordering; interior = box + 50 mm in each direction.
  • Odor control of the vented design is untested; a carbon filter pad in the vents is an add-on, not modeled.
  • A French cleat is not offered: a litter box is too heavy and wet for a cleat; the piece stands on short feet raised for the robot vacuum instead.
  • Exterior (marine) plywood and cut quality of sealed inside faces are unverified.
  • Price band is my estimate, not a market quote.
