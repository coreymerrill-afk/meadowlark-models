Low TV Console / Media Cabinet

A long, low media console with open bays, rear cable slots and a cleat-ready rail, raised so the robot vacuum goes underneath. Cut to the width of your wall.

Build stages

  1. 1

    Cut the sheet(s) about 186 min (3 h 6 min)

    Run the fit-test coupon first (slot = tab + 0.1 mm), then cut 1 sheet(s) from the DXFs (CUT layer): 28 m of path. Inside-out order, air assist on. The 1500 mm-class TOP and BOTTOM are the longest parts on the bed.

  2. 2

    Remove char and sand about 55 min

    Brush char from the cut edges, 120 then 180 grit on faces and edges; ease the top corners. Wipe with a damp rag.

  3. 3

    Dry fit about 15 min

    Stand SIDE-L and SIDE-R, push BOTTOM, the 2 divider(s) and both rails into place, then lower TOP over all the tabs. Check square and that TOP sits flat. No glue yet.

  4. 4

    Pre-finish the inside faces about 45 min

    Seal / oil the bay faces and the rail faces before glue-up (mask tabs and slots so the glue still bonds). Two coats on all faces is easiest done flat.

  5. 5

    Glue-up (Titebond III) about 35 min

    Lay SIDE-L flat. Glue and tap in BOTTOM, the divider(s), RAIL-BOT and RAIL-TOP, fit SIDE-R and clamp. Last, seat TOP on every tab with a few light taps from a block. Work in two halves if alone (10 minute open time).

  6. 6

    Screws as clamps about 20 min

    10 #8 x 1-1/2 in screws (3.0 mm pilots cut in): through each side face into the BOTTOM edge, and down through TOP into the side and divider edges. Countersink and plug the top screws.

  7. 7

    Cable slots, TV strap, optional cleat about 25 min

    Feed cables through the 3 rear slots. Fit the TV anti-tip strap through the 6 mm holes in RAIL-TOP into a stud. Optional wall-hung version: Z-bar cleat across RAIL-TOP into studs; heavy (rated load unverified), so only with a proper stud-rated cleat and an empty-console test first.

  8. 8

    Finish, test, pack about 45 min

    Two coats of water-based poly or hardwax oil, 24 h cure, 40 kg weight test on TOP, felt pads under the 4 feet, flat-pack in one 1.55 x 0.45 x 0.15 m box with corner guards.

About 7 h 6 min for all 8 stages.

Safety notes

  • Strap the TV to the wall (anti-tip strap); a low console does not stop a TV tipping off it.
  • Rated target 40 kg TV (88 lb) on the top, UNVERIFIED - load test one unit to 60 kg before selling; keep each bay <= 650 mm.
  • Not a clothing-storage unit (STURDY Act applies to dressers >= 27 in); not a children's product (no CPSIA testing).
  • Wall-hung (cleat) use only into studs with a stud-rated cleat; test empty then loaded.
  • Keep AV gear ventilated: the open bays and rear slots are for that; do not box it in.

Unverified / to test

  • Machine time is an estimate (120 W diode on 18 mm ply, sourced range, not measured on this machine); cut time and profit/hour depend on it.
  • Top sag is a hand calculation (assumed ply modulus); a long span with a heavy TV needs a physical load test.
  • Wall-hung cleat capacity of an aluminum Z-bar on a console this size is unverified.
  • No doors or drawers are modeled; the TOP overhangs 20 mm on every side.
  • Price band is my estimate, not a market quote.
