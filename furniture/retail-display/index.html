Build stages

  1. 1

    Cut the sheets about 207 min (3 h 27 min)

    Fit-test coupon first (slot = tab + 0.1 mm); cut 2 sheet(s): 31 m of path. Replace the logo placeholder in sheet 1 (CUT layer) with the customer artwork: keep every letter island tied with a 3 mm bridge. Inside-out order, air assist on.

  2. 2

    Remove char and sand about 60 min (1 h)

    Brush off the char, 120 then 180 grit; ease the shelf front edges and the logo opening. Wipe clean.

  3. 3

    Dry fit about 20 min

    Lay SIDE-L flat, push the 4 shelves, 2 braces and the sign band into their slots, add SIDE-R. Check the tab ends are flush and that the unit stands without rocking.

  4. 4

    Finish all parts flat about 60 min (1 h)

    Oil or poly all faces and edges flat (fast), mask the tab and slot surfaces only if you want them to be glued later. Brand colour or stain on the sign band if ordered.

  5. 5

    Test-assemble with screws about 20 min

    NO GLUE: it is knock-down. Drive 12 #8 x 1-1/2 in screws (3.0 mm pilots pre-cut) through the side faces into the shelf and brace edges. Re-removable for flat storage.

  6. 6

    Stability check about 15 min

    Tip test with every shelf loaded (see the load rating): it must not tip at a 10 deg lean. Fit the 2 anchor holes in the sign band with a wall strap or hang a 5 kg ballast bag on the bottom shelf for outdoor use.

  7. 7

    Knock down and flat-pack about 25 min

    Unscrew, stack the 4 shelves, braces and band between the two sides with cardboard spacers, add the screws in a labelled bag and the 1-page assembly sheet; 1.5 m x 0.5 m x 0.2 m box.

  8. 8

    Customer assembly (about 10 min) about 10 min

    Customer lays one side flat, slides in shelves and braces, adds the second side and drives the 12 screws with a Phillips/Torx driver. No glue, no clamps.

About 6 h 57 min for all 8 stages.

Safety notes

  • Target load per shelf 15 kg, bottom shelf 25 kg - UNVERIFIED; load-test one unit.
  • Tall and shallow at the top: strap to a wall indoors or add 5 kg ballast outdoors (wind). Do not let customers climb it.
  • Keep heavy product on the bottom tiers; the top tier is for light items only.
  • Not a children's product.

Unverified / to test

  • Load rating and tip angle untested.
  • Folding variant is not modeled (this is knock-down only).
  • Laser feed 100-200 mm/min is an estimate.
  • Logo cut-out is a placeholder; customer artwork needs bridges for letter islands.
  • Price band is an estimate from SHORTLIST.md (the repeat B2B reorders are the draw, not a measured demand).
