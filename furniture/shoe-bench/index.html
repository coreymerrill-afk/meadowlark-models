Entryway Shoe Bench

A seat plus shoe storage in one flat-pack: slatted shelves, a laser-cut cushion recess, raised so the robot vacuum goes underneath.

1,000 mm wide, 400 mm deep, 450 mm tall (39.4 × 15.7 × 17.7 in). 90 mm clear underneath. The top overhangs 20 mm. Shelf bay 134 mm clear. 1 sheet. Mid retail price $220, an estimate. Typical machine time 2.4 h.

Build stages

  1. 1

    Cut the sheet(s) about 240 min (4 h)

    Run the fit-test coupon (slot = tab + 0.1 mm), then cut 1 sheet(s) from the DXFs (CUT layer): 36 m of path, 33 slotted vents in the shelves. Inside-out order, air assist on.

  2. 2

    Remove char and sand about 55 min

    Brush the char from every cut edge, 120 then 180 grit; ease the seat edge to a 3 mm round-over by hand; sand the vent slots with a folded strip of paper.

  3. 3

    Dry fit about 15 min

    Stand the SIDES, slide in the 2 SHELF board(s), dividers, RAIL-TOP, then the SEAT. Check tab ends are flush and the case is square; no glue.

  4. 4

    Pre-finish about 45 min

    Seal all faces now (poly or hardwax oil) - shoes are wet and dirty. Mask the tabs and slots so the glue still bonds.

  5. 5

    Glue-up (Titebond III) about 30 min

    Glue the dividers into the shelves, then the shelves into SIDE-L, add SIDE-R, RAIL-TOP, and finally seat the SEAT over all top tabs. Clamp across the width; wipe squeeze-out.

  6. 6

    Screws as clamps about 20 min

    6 #8 screws (3.0 mm pilots pre-cut): #8 x 1-1/2 in through the side faces into the shelf edges and down through the seat into the side edges (hidden under the frame); then 6 x #8 x 1-1/4 in down through SEAT-RIM into the seat.

  7. 7

    Cushion frame about 15 min

    Glue and screw SEAT-RIM on the seat (covers the tab ends). The recess holds a cushion up to about 45 mm thick, size = opening printed on sheet 1. Cut the cushion board from the offcut if wanted.

  8. 8

    Optional cleat / wall hang about 20 min

    For a floating SHOE SHELF version only: Z-bar cleat on the rear face of RAIL-TOP into studs. Do not use as a seat when wall-hung unless every cleat screw is in a stud and the wall half is rated (unverified).

  9. 9

    Finish, load test, pack about 40 min

    Final coat on the seat, 24 h cure, sit-test with 120 kg, felt pads on the 4 feet, flat-pack in one box with corner guards.

About 8 h for all 9 stages.

Materials and hardware

Safety notes

  • Seat load rating target 120 kg (265 lb) - UNVERIFIED, load test every design change (sag test at the seat centre).
  • The center divider(s) carry the seat; do not omit them on benches wider than 800 mm.
  • Wall-hung (cleat) use: shoe shelf only unless studs and a rated cleat are used.
  • Not a children's product (no CPSIA testing).

Unverified / to test

  • Seat strength and deflection untested (18 mm ply over 1000 mm without dividers would sag about 12 mm at 100 kg by hand calculation).
  • Machine time is an estimate (120 W diode on 18 mm ply, sourced range, not measured on this machine); cut time and profit/hour depend on it.
  • Cushion fit depends on the cushion bought.
  • Composite decking top is a CNC-only variant, not modeled here.
  • Price band is an estimate from SHORTLIST.md.
