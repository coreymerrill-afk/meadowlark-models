Build stages

  1. 1

    Cut the sheet about 231 min (3 h 51 min)

    Run the fit-test coupon (slot = tab + 0.1 mm), then cut 1 sheet from the DXF (CUT layer): 35 m of path, 68 drainage slots. Inside-out order, air assist on. If the ply is exterior/marine grade, expect the phenolic glue lines to char more: slow down and test a scrap.

  2. 2

    Remove char and sand about 40 min

    Brush char from every edge, 120 then 180 grit; ease the shelf edges. Sand the drainage slots with a folded strip of paper.

  3. 3

    Dry fit about 12 min

    Lay SIDE-L flat, push the 3 SHELF boards and the two rails into its slots, add SIDE-R. Check each shelf tab ends flush with the outside face and the stair profile lines up. No glue.

  4. 4

    Pre-finish about 60 min (1 h)

    Seal every face and edge now: 3 coats of spar urethane or exterior poly (wet pots, spills). Mask tabs and slots so the glue still bonds. Seal the drainage slot edges well; they take the water.

  5. 5

    Glue-up (Titebond III, water-resistant) about 25 min

    Glue the 3 shelf tabs and the two rail tabs into SIDE-L, then fit SIDE-R and clamp across the width. Wipe squeeze-out at once; check square by measuring both diagonals.

  6. 6

    Screws as clamps about 12 min

    10 #8 screws (3.0 mm pilots pre-cut): #8 x 1-1/2 in through the side faces into the shelf edges, between the tabs. Use coated or stainless screws if it will live outdoors.

  7. 7

    Anti-tip anchor about 10 min

    Fit the anti-tip strap through one of the two 6 mm holes in RAIL-TOP into a wall stud. Heavy pots on the top tier raise the tipping risk, so keep the heaviest pots on the bottom tier.

  8. 8

    Final coat, cure, pack about 30 min

    Last coat on the top faces, 24 h cure, 15 kg pot load test on each shelf, felt/rubber pads on the 4 feet, flat-pack in one box (about 720 x 820 x 80 mm).

About 7 h for all 8 stages.

Safety notes

  • Anchor to a wall stud with the anti-tip strap (ASTM F3096 style); mandatory above 900 mm, recommended on every unit.
  • Rated target 15 kg per shelf (UNVERIFIED - load test a shelf before selling). Wet soil is heavy: 5 L of damp potting mix is about 4 kg.
  • Not a children's product (no CPSIA testing). Not a climbing aid.
  • Outdoor use needs exterior-grade ply plus a UV-rated finish; indoor-grade birch ply swells and delaminates when wet.

Unverified / to test

  • Machine time is an estimate (120 W diode on 18 mm ply, sourced range, not measured on this machine); cut time and profit/hour depend on it.
  • Shelf load rating and deflection untested; the deflection line in checks.txt is a hand calculation with an assumed ply modulus.
  • Exterior/marine ply (phenolic glue) cut quality on the diode laser is unverified.
  • The cost model charges one whole 4x8 sheet but this design fills only 34% of it (offcut not credited). Nesting 2 stands per sheet (area allows it; not nested here) would cut mid cost to about $83 and lift mid gross margin from 22% to about 49%. As modeled, the low price band is below cost.
  • Price band is my estimate, not a market quote.
