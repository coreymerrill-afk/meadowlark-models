Build stages

  1. 1

    Cut the sheets about 261 min (4 h 21 min)

    Fit-test coupon first (slot = tab + 0.1 mm), then cut 1 sheet(s): 39 m of cut path. The 1200 x 600 TOP is the biggest part; keep it flat on the honeycomb and cut inside-out.

  2. 2

    Remove char and sand about 70 min (1 h 10 min)

    Brush char off every edge, 120 then 180 grit; round the desktop edge by hand (about 3 mm). Sand the side-panel windows.

  3. 3

    Dry fit the frame about 20 min

    Stand SIDE-L and SIDE-R, slide the BACK-STRETCHER into both, add the DRAWER-DIVIDER and FRONT-APRON, then drop the TOP over all the tabs. Check the tab ends are flush with the desktop and the legs stand square.

  4. 4

    Dry fit drawer about 20 min

    Assemble the drawer (finger corners, bottom with 4 tabs, face) and slide it on the two LEDGERs; it should run freely with 1.5 mm side play. Sand the finger pull slots.

  5. 5

    Pre-finish about 50 min

    Oil or poly the desktop top, the drawer, and the inside faces now; mask the tabs and slots so the glue bonds.

  6. 6

    Glue-up frame (Titebond III) about 35 min

    Glue the stretcher, apron, divider and ledgers into SIDE-L/R and the divider, then fit the TOP onto the tabs and clamp across the width. Wipe squeeze-out; 30 min clamp, 24 h before heavy use.

  7. 7

    Screws as clamps about 25 min

    10 #8 x 1-1/2 in and 2 #8 x 1-1/4 in screws (3.0 mm pilots pre-cut): through each side face into the back stretcher edge; the drawer corners, bottom and face as the nightstand drawer.

  8. 8

    Monitor shelf and cable slot about 15 min

    Glue MON-UP-L/R into their top slots (they stand on the desktop and their tabs come through underneath), then the MON-SHELF onto their upper tabs. Edge-band or sand the cable slot.

  9. 9

    Wall stabilizer / cleat (optional) about 15 min

    Screw a Z-bar half to the rear face of the BACK-STRETCHER and the other half into studs to stop the desk creeping or wobbling. It is a stabilizer, not a floating-desk mount.

  10. 10

    Finish and pack about 40 min

    Last coat, 24 h cure, felt pads under the 4 feet, load-test 60 kg on the desktop centre, flat-pack with corner guards (parts up to 1.2 m).

About 9 h 11 min for all 10 stages.

Safety notes

  • Rated target 60 kg distributed on the desktop - UNVERIFIED; sag-test a 1200 mm top with 20 kg at the centre.
  • Top is held only by through-tabs and glue: let the glue cure 24 h before loading.
  • Stand-height (1000 mm) builds are less stable: fit the wall stabilizer and widen the stance (depth >= 650).
  • Not a children's product.

Unverified / to test

  • Desktop deflection with 18 mm ply over 1200 mm not tested; rear stretcher and divider are the only stiffeners.
  • Machine time is an estimate (120 W diode on 18 mm ply, sourced range, not measured on this machine); cut time and profit/hour depend on it.
  • Visible tab ends on the desktop surface (sand flush / cover with a desk mat or veneer).
  • Drawer slides on plywood ledgers (no hardware slides).
  • Price band is an estimate from SHORTLIST.md.
