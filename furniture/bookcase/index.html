Build stages

  1. 1

    Cut the sheets about 216 min (3 h 36 min)

    Load sheet 1 on the honeycomb bed, run the fit-test coupon first (slot = tab + 0.1 mm), then cut sheet 1 and sheet 2 from the DXFs (CUT layer). 1 sheet(s), 32 m of cut path; keep the air assist on and the cut order inside-out (holes and slots first).

  2. 2

    Remove char and sand about 60 min (1 h)

    Pop parts out, brush the char from the cut edges, 120 then 180 grit on faces and edges; ease the top and shelf corners. Wipe with a damp rag.

  3. 3

    Dry fit about 15 min

    Stand SIDE-L and SIDE-R, push BOTTOM and MID into their slots, then TOP, then the two rails. Check square, check each tab ends flush with the outside face. No glue yet.

  4. 4

    Finish the loose parts first about 45 min

    Seal / oil all 3 ADJ-SHELF boards and the inside faces before glue-up (glue will not stick over finish, so mask the tabs and slots).

  5. 5

    Glue-up (Titebond III) about 30 min

    Lay SIDE-L flat. Glue and tap in BOTTOM, MID, the 1 divider(s), RAIL-BOT and RAIL-TOP, then fit SIDE-R and clamp. Last: seat TOP on all tabs. Wipe squeeze-out at once. Work time about 10 minutes, so do it in two halves if alone.

  6. 6

    Screw-clamp the joints about 15 min

    6 #8 screws (3.0 mm pilots are already cut): #8 x 1-1/2 in through each side face into the BOTTOM and MID edges, and down through the TOP into the side edges; countersink and plug the 4 top screws. Square the case while the glue sets.

  7. 7

    Cleat and anti-tip anchor about 25 min

    Screw the unit half of an aluminum Z-bar (1 in) across the rear face of RAIL-TOP (it sits in the 20 mm rear recess). Mount the wall half level into studs. ALWAYS also fit an anti-tip strap: screw it through one of the two 6 mm holes in RAIL-TOP into a stud. Required on every unit above 900 mm tall.

  8. 8

    Finish about 40 min

    Two coats of water-based poly or hardwax oil, 180-grit between coats; 24 h to cure before packing.

  9. 9

    Shelf pins, test, pack about 25 min

    Press in the shelf pins (rows at [928.0, 1152.0, 1376.0] mm above the floor; 23 pin rows available at 32 mm pitch), load-test with 20 kg per shelf, then flat-pack: 1 sheets of parts in one 1.3 x 0.4 x 0.25 m box with corner guards.

About 7 h 51 min for all 9 stages.

Safety notes

  • Anchor to a wall stud with an anti-tip strap on every unit (ASTM F3096 style); mandatory above 900 mm.
  • Not a clothing-storage unit (STURDY Act applies to dressers >= 27 in); not a children's product (no CPSIA testing).
  • Rated target 20 kg per 800 mm shelf (UNVERIFIED - load test one shelf to 40 kg before selling).
  • Keep depth >= 0.15 x height; heavy items low.

Unverified / to test

  • Laser feed 100-200 mm/min on 18 mm ply is an estimate; cut time and profit/hour depend on it.
  • Shelf load rating and deflection not tested (18 mm ply, 800 mm span).

  • Optional 1/4 in back panel not modeled or nested; rails give racking strength only with glue.
