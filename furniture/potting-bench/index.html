Garden Potting Bench

A potting bench with a drainage worktop, a lower shelf and a hutch shelf with tool-hang holes, built from exterior-grade ply and raised off wet ground. Cut to your patio or greenhouse.

1,100 mm wide, 500 mm deep, 1,500 mm tall (43.3 × 19.7 × 59.1 in). Worktop at 900 mm. 60 mm clear under the feet. Upper shelf 217 mm deep. Lower bay 503 mm wide. 2 sheets. Mid retail price $420, an estimate. Typical machine time 3.6 h.

Build stages

  1. 1

    Cut the sheets about 347 min (5 h 47 min)

    Run the fit-test coupon first (slot = tab + 0.1 mm; do not tighten it for outdoor ply, which swells), then cut 2 sheets from the DXFs (CUT layer): 52 m of path, 87 drainage slots, 13 tool-hang holes. Inside-out order, air assist on. Exterior-grade ply has more glue lines: expect more char, and test a scrap.

  2. 2

    Remove char and sand about 60 min (1 h)

    Brush the char from every edge, 120 then 180 grit; round over the worktop edge. End grain drinks water: sand the slot edges smooth now so the sealer can soak in.

  3. 3

    Dry fit about 20 min

    Stand SIDE-L flat, push in SHELF-LOW, the 1 divider(s), BACK and SHELF-UP, add SIDE-R, then lower the TOP over the uprights and every tab. Check square. No glue.

  4. 4

    Seal every face and edge about 120 min (2 h)

    Outdoors the finish is the product: 3 coats of exterior spar urethane, or 2 coats of penetrating epoxy sealer then 2 coats of exterior paint, on every face and edge, including the insides of the drainage slots. Mask the tabs and slots so the glue still bonds (a dry run first). Do this before glue-up.

  5. 5

    Glue-up (Titebond III, water-resistant) about 40 min

    Glue SHELF-LOW, the divider(s), BACK and SHELF-UP into SIDE-L, fit SIDE-R and clamp. Then lower the TOP over the tabs and the two uprights. Wipe squeeze-out at once. Titebond III is rated water-resistant (exterior use under cover); it is not for standing water.

  6. 6

    Screws as clamps about 20 min

    6 #8 x 1-1/2 in stainless or coated deck screws (3.0 mm pilots cut in): through the side faces into the lower shelf edges and down through the TOP into the side edges (plug and seal the heads). For a knock-down bench omit the glue and use the screws only (weaker; unverified).

  7. 7

    Wall anchor about 15 min

    Strap the BACK to the wall through one of the two 6 mm holes near its top (stud or masonry anchor). Required: this piece is 1500 mm tall and holds wet soil.

  8. 8

    Hooks, test, pack about 45 min

    Fit S-hooks or 10 mm dowel pegs in the tool-hang holes. Load-test 40 kg on the worktop and 25 kg on the lower shelf. Feet pads on all 4 feet. Flat-pack in two boxes (long parts about 1.55 m).

About 11 h 7 min for all 8 stages.

Materials and hardware

Safety notes

  • Anchor the BACK to the wall (anti-tip strap or bracket); mandatory, the bench is 1500 mm tall and carries wet soil.
  • Rated target 40 kg on the worktop and 25 kg on the lower shelf, UNVERIFIED - load test before selling; wet soil is about 1.3 kg per litre.
  • Use exterior-grade ply only; interior ply delaminates outdoors within a season. Seal every edge.
  • Not a children's product (no CPSIA testing); do not use as a ladder or step.
  • Keep it off standing water and treat the feet pads as wear parts.

Unverified / to test

  • Machine time is an estimate (120 W diode on 18 mm ply, sourced range, not measured on this machine); cut time and profit/hour depend on it.
  • Exterior and marine plywood (phenolic/waterproof glue lines) cut quality and speed on the 120 W diode are unverified; the laser may need more passes or leave heavier char.
  • Weather life of the finish and of the tab joints is untested; swelling can bind tabs, so the 0.1 mm slot clearance should not be tightened.
  • A folding version is not modeled; knock-down (screws, no glue) is possible but unverified and weaker.
  • French cleat not offered: the piece is heavy, wet and meant to stand on its feet.
  • Price band is my estimate, not a market quote. Low and high bands are applied to the default-size cost, so the low band is about break-even; a balcony-size unit costs less in material.
