Nightstand

A drawer-and-shelf nightstand that fits on a single 4x8 sheet, stands on its own feet so the robot vacuum goes underneath, and converts to a wall-hung French-cleat piece.

560 mm wide, 400 mm deep, 600 mm tall (22 × 15.7 × 23.6 in). 100 mm clear underneath. Foot notch 86 mm. The top overhangs 20 mm. 1 sheet. Mid retail price $190, an estimate.

Build stages

  1. 1

    Cut the sheet about 173 min (2 h 53 min)

    Cut the fit-test coupon first (slot = tab + 0.1 mm), then the sheet from the DXF (CUT layer): 26 m of path. Cut small holes and slots first, outlines last; air assist on.

  2. 2

    Remove char and sand about 45 min

    Brush the char off every cut edge, then 120 and 180 grit. Ease the top edges by hand. Sand the drawer pull slot.

  3. 3

    Dry fit about 15 min

    Stand SIDE-L and SIDE-R, push in the SHELF and BACK tabs, then the TOP. Check every tab is flush and the carcass is square. No glue yet.

  4. 4

    Pre-finish about 40 min

    Stain or seal every face now (oil or poly). Mask the tabs and slots so the glue bonds to bare wood.

  5. 5

    Drawer box about 25 min

    Glue the finger-jointed DRW-SIDE/FRONT/BACK corners, drop in DRW-BOTTOM through its side tabs, and drive the 8 corner and bottom screws (3.0 mm pilots are pre-cut). Check for square.

  6. 6

    Glue-up (Titebond III) about 25 min

    Glue the SHELF and BACK into SIDE-L, add SIDE-R, then set the TOP over all four side tabs. Clamp across the width; wipe squeeze-out.

  7. 7

    Screws as clamps about 25 min

    30 #8 screws in all: #8 x 1-1/2 in through the side faces into the shelf, back and top edges (hidden at the sides, heads on top get filled); #8 x 1-1/4 in for the drawer runners and face. Pilots are laser-cut.

  8. 8

    Runners and drawer face about 20 min

    Screw the two RUNNERs to the inside of the sides from the outside, drop the drawer on them, then fix DRW-FACE with a 1.5 mm reveal from inside the drawer front.

  9. 9

    Optional French cleat about 20 min

    For a wall-hung version: glue CLEAT-BACKER-A and -B to the rear face of BACK (before the drawer goes in), screw an aluminum Z-bar to layer B, and put the wall half into two studs. Skip for floor standing.

  10. 10

    Finish and pack about 30 min

    Final coat, 24 h cure, felt dots under the 4 feet, flat-pack the drawer parts with the carcass in one box.

About 6 h 58 min for all 10 stages.

Safety notes

  • Not a tall piece: no wall anchor is required, but if the cleat version is used, both wall-half screws must land in studs.
  • Drawer has no stop or soft-close: the box can pull out fully. Fit a drawer stop if used with children.
  • Wall-hung load: keep contents under 10 kg. Seller-claimed cleat rating is not verified.
  • Not a children's product (no CPSIA testing).

Unverified / to test

  • Laser feed 100-200 mm/min is an estimate.
  • Wall-hung strength is untested (cleat rating is the seller's claim).
  • Price band is my assumption (nightstand is not in SHORTLIST.md).
  • Robot-vacuum clearance: 90 mm min is a typical-robot guess, check your unit.
  • Parts are tuned to 18 mm ply; depth range 340-480 was build-checked only at 360/400/440 mm.
